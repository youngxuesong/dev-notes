# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 2026**

Welcome to DevNotes ("we", "our", or "the site"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect and process information when you visit our website.

## 1. Information We Collect

As a static technology blog, we do not require user account registration, nor do we directly harvest your personal identification information.

### 1.1 Web Server Logs & Analytics
When you browse this site, our hosting infrastructure and CDN providers (such as Cloudflare) automatically collect standard connection metadata, including your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, timestamp, and requested URLs. This data is used solely for security defense and aggregate traffic analytics.

### 1.2 Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising
- We use **Google AdSense** to serve advertisements across our site.
- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses **cookies** to serve ads on our site based on prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).

## 2. Data Security
We implement strict HTTPS / TLS transport encryption across the entire website to protect data in transit.
